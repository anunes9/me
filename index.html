About

Hi, I'm André. A developer from Lisbon who likes to know how things are made and why.

I started mobile, learned the backend because the project needed it, and grew into leading teams. These days I sit between clients and engineers, and I like it there: close enough to the idea to shape it, close enough to the code to ship it.
